# Delete your Dabba Diary account and data

Dabba Diary (developed by Rutik Rathod, contact: rathodrutik05@gmail.com) lets you delete your account and cloud backup in two ways.

## In the app

1. Open Dabba Diary and go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Backup** and tap **Delete account & cloud backup**.
3. Confirm. Google asks you to sign in again before an account can be deleted.

This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account and every record backed up under it: your daily delivered and skipped marks, your name, price, start date, meal choices and settings.

## Without the app

Email rathodrutik05@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Dabba Diary data". Your account and cloud backup are deleted within 30 days, and you get a confirmation reply.

## What gets deleted

- Your Firebase Authentication account record (your Google account email and name as held by sign-in).
- Every record in your cloud backup.

## What stays

- Data on your phone is not touched by this process. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove it.
- If you never signed in, there is no account or cloud data to delete; everything exists only on your device.
- Advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
